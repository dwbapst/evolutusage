# Evolutus 
## Foraminifera Evolution/Ecology Simulator

### Authors: 
### P. Topa, J. Tyszka, M. Komosinski, Ł. Faber, A. Mensfelt, J. Goleń, M. Bassara

See these references for details and technical specifications:

- Topa, P., Faber, Ł., Tyszka, J., & Komosinski, M. (2017). Modelling ecology and evolution of Foraminifera in the agent-oriented distributed platform. Journal of Computational Science, 18, 69-84.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1877750316301168

- Komosinski, M., Mensfelt, A., Tyszka, J., & Goleń, J. (2017). Multi-agent simulation of benthic foraminifera response to annual variability of feeding fluxes. Journal of Computational Science, 21, 419-431.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1877750316301715

- Topa, P., Komosinski, M., Bassara, M., & Tyszka, J. (2016). eVolutus: a configurable platform designed for ecological and evolutionary experiments tested on Foraminifera. In Man–Machine Interactions 4 (pp. 269-278). Springer, Cham.
https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-23437-3_23
